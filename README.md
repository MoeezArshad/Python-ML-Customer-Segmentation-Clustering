# Python-ML-Customer-Segmentation-Clustering
This project applies K-Means clustering to segment mall customers based on age, income, and spending score — final clusters are visualized below:

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/18326ef5-5790-4e72-bb03-abca20b92673)


# Key Dimensions and Metrics

- <b>Annual Income (k$):</b> Customer’s annual income (range ~$15k–$137k, mean ≈$60k) used to gauge spending power.
- <b>Spending Score (1–100):</b> Mall Shopping Score indicating spending propensity (range 1–99, mean ≈50).
- <b>Clustering Method:</b> K-Means clustering (optimal ~5 clusters, determined via elbow method) to group customers by these features.

# Summary of Insights

- <b>Cluster 1 (High-Income & High-Spending):</b>
This segment has the highest average income and the highest shopping scores. They are the most lucrative cluster. Marketing focus on this segment can drive revenue growth (e.g. exclusive loyalty offers or new-product promotions).​

- <b>Cluster 4 (Low Income & High-Spending):</b>
This segment has the lowest income, but still one of the highest spending scores among the clusters. 
These customers respond well to flexible offers (e.g. buy-now-pay-later financing) and trendy product promotions suited to their age group​

- <b>Low-Spending Customers (Across Income Levels):</b>
Customers with low spending scores (regardless of income) are less active shoppers. They contribute less to sales on average.This group may only respond to deep discounts or loyalty incentives. Given their lower ROI, they are a lower priority for aggressive marketing​

# Recommendations & Next Steps

- <b>Focus on High-Value Segments:</b> Prioritize marketing and retention programs for the high-income, high-spending cluster – they have the greatest revenue potential​. For example, tailor premium offers or loyalty perks to their profile.
- <b>Engage Low-Income High-Spenders:</b> Develop campaigns and financing options (e.g. installment plans)  to boost conversions. Their lower income and high engagement suggest value in flexible payment promotions.
- <b>Smart Promotions for Low Spenders:</b> Use selective discounts or engagement tactics for low-spending clusters, but focus resources where ROI justifies it​
